# Customer Churn Intelligence Platform

## Business Insights

### 1. Overall Customer Churn
- Total Customers: 5,000
- Churned Customers: 1,710
- Churn Rate: 34.20%

The project shows that 34.20% of customers have churned. This indicates a significant customer retention challenge.

### 2. Contract Type
Contract type is an important factor associated with customer churn.

Customers with shorter-term contracts should be monitored closely because they can be more likely to leave compared with customers on longer-term contracts.

### 3. City Analysis
Churn rates vary across different cities.

City-wise churn analysis can help the business identify locations where customer retention strategies may need improvement.

### 4. Payment Method
Churn rates differ across payment methods.

The business can investigate whether certain payment methods are associated with higher churn and improve the customer payment experience.

### 5. Internet Service
Internet service type is another factor related to churn.

Customers using different internet services show different churn behaviour, which can help the company identify service-related retention opportunities.

### 6. Churn Reasons
Churn reasons help identify why customers are leaving.

Major reasons such as service problems, high prices, support issues, low usage, and competitors can be used to design targeted retention strategies.

### 7. Revenue at Risk
Revenue at Risk represents the monthly charges associated with customers who have already churned.

This metric helps management understand the financial impact of customer churn.

### 8. Satisfaction Score
Customer satisfaction is strongly relevant to retention.

Customers with lower satisfaction scores should receive higher priority for customer support and retention campaigns.

### 9. Support Tickets
A higher number of support tickets can indicate customer dissatisfaction or service problems.

Customers with frequent support issues should be monitored proactively.

### 10. Tenure
Customer tenure helps identify the relationship between customer age and churn.

Newer customers should receive additional onboarding and engagement support to improve retention.

### 11. Monthly Charges
Monthly charges can influence customer churn.

Customers with higher monthly charges can be considered a potential high-risk segment and can be targeted with suitable plans or offers.

## Key Business Recommendations

1. Focus retention campaigns on high-risk customers.
2. Improve service quality and customer support.
3. Monitor customers with low satisfaction scores.
4. Investigate customers with frequent support tickets.
5. Provide better offers for price-sensitive customers.
6. Focus on retaining new customers during their early tenure.
7. Monitor high-value customers with high monthly charges.
8. Use churn dashboards for regular management monitoring.

## Project Conclusion

The Customer Churn Intelligence Platform uses Python, SQL and Power BI to analyse customer behaviour, identify churn drivers and highlight revenue at risk.

The analysis can help businesses take proactive customer retention decisions based on data.
